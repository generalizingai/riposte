Riposte

Drafts replies worth sending, in your voice. Nothing posts automatically.

API key

Stored unencrypted in this browser profile's extension storage and sent only to api.anthropic.com. Anyone with access to this Mac account can read it. Use a key scoped to this tool so you can revoke it on its own.

Generation

Your voice

Paste 15 to 30 of your own posts, one per line. This is what stops the replies reading like everyone else's AI. Topics do not matter, only how you write.

What you can speak to

Used only by the review queue, to decide which posts are worth your reply. Be specific and include the unglamorous parts. "Shipped three SaaS products, one pulled 14k visitors and zero revenue" is far more useful than "startups and marketing".
